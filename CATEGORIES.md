## How crossover books are counted

- Science fiction includes Red Rising, the Void Trilogy, and the Infinite Timeline despite fantasy or thriller elements. Four Minutes and Quantum Radio are counted here for their speculative premises.
- Convergence is urban fantasy and is counted with fantasy, alongside Lightbringer and The Age of Madness.
- Beneath the Dark Ice is counted as supernatural suspense; Kill Decision is counted as a techno-thriller.
- The Iliad and The Three Musketeers are literary classics, not nonfiction history.
- History includes The Making of the Atomic Bomb (cataloged as Making of the Atomic Bomb), Titan II, and Nuclear War: A Scenario; the last is forward-looking risk analysis rather than a historical narrative.
- Educational is a narrow category: Norms and Nobility, The Consuming Instinct, How to Lie with Statistics, and Super Crunchers. It is not an umbrella total for all nonfiction.

The assignments are editorial judgments informed by book content, series, and publisher descriptions; they are not a verbatim export of Audible taxonomy. For example, the [Convergence description](https://www.audible.com/pd/B09ZZ8VMKL) calls it urban fantasy, the [Four Minutes publisher description](https://www.blackstonepublishing.com/products/book-edrn) describes its speculative premise, and [Beneath the Dark Ice](https://www.audible.com/pd/B0065717C2) is presented as supernatural suspense.
